        How words become a plant

        • |Every word adds a node to the stem and sends out a side branch. Longer words grow longer.
        • aEvery vowel becomes a leaf, and the vowel sets the leaf's shape. Capitalised words grow broader leaves.
        • .A word ending in . or ! flowers. An exclamation mark gives a double flower. A ? curls into a tendril, and , ; : set a bud.
        • 7Numbers ripen into berries.
        • ↓Every sentence adds a root. A line that ends without punctuation keeps its top bud closed.
        • ♪Turn on sound to hear it grow. Each word plucks a low note, each leaf chimes on its vowel's pitch, and flowers ring a chord. The key comes from the whole line.
