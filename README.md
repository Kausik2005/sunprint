# Verse Garden

Type a line and it grows into a plant. The same words always grow the same plant, printed as a cyanotype (the old blue sun-print process) and given a made-up Latin name.

**Play it:** https://claude.ai/artifact/8Hgv9WXDpyV6MGWf5tQRBd

## How words become a plant

- Every word adds a node to the stem and sends out a side branch. Longer words grow longer.
- Every vowel becomes a leaf, and the vowel sets its shape: *a* ovate, *e* lanceolate, *i* linear, *o* round, *u* heart, *y* paired needles. Capitalised words grow broader leaves.
- A word ending in `.` flowers, `!` gives a double flower, `?` curls into a tendril, and `, ; :` set a bud.
- Numbers ripen into berries. Every sentence adds a root.
- About one line in 24 prints tea-toned: brown and cream instead of blue.

## Features

- **Daily commission:** a word puzzle. Grow a plant that meets three requirements in six growths or fewer, keep a streak, and copy a shareable result grid.
- **Field journal:** 18 traits to discover, with hints.
- **Herbarium:** press plants you like to keep them (saved in your browser).
- **Share links:** every plant has its own link.
- **Wind and sound:** plants sway, and each line plays its own tune as it grows.
- **Shared garden:** everyone with the page plants lines in one garden that updates live.
- Light, dark and auto themes.

## Running it

It's a single file, `index.html`, with no build step. Open it in a browser, or serve it with GitHub Pages.

The **shared garden** only works on the claude.ai page, because it uses claude.ai's shared storage. Everywhere else it shows a note saying so, and everything else works.
