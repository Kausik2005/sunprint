# Sunprint

A wake-up tracker with a plant in it. Pick a wake-up window, prove you're up in time each morning, and your plant grows. Miss a morning and it starts to drop its leaves. The plant is drawn as a cyanotype, the old blue sun-print process.

**Use it:** https://kausik2005.github.io/verse-garden/

Or on claude.ai, where your plant syncs across devices: https://claude.ai/artifact/8Hgv9WXDpyV6MGWf5tQRBd

## How it works

1. **Plant a seed.** Choose a window, like 6:30 to 7:30 AM, and the days to track. Days you leave out are rest days and don't count either way.
2. **Prove you're up** during your window, in one of three ways:
   - **Quote:** type out a short quote. Pasting is blocked.
   - **Photo:** take a photo. It has to be from the last 10 minutes. It stays on your device; only the time is saved.
   - **Sum:** solve a sum in your head.
3. **Your plant grows.** Each morning you make it adds a node to the stem. Three in a row set a bud, and seven in a row flower. It's fully grown at 40 mornings.
4. **Miss a morning and it sheds.** About a fifth of the leaves fall to the soil, the streak resets, and a tendril marks where it broke. Each morning you're back grows some leaves again.

A schedule change only starts with your next window, so it can't rescue a morning already under way.

## Also

- Streak, best streak, mornings, and on-time rate
- A calendar of the last five weeks
- Wind, sound, and light, dark and auto themes

## Running it

It's a single file, `index.html`, with no build step. Open it in a browser, or use the GitHub Pages site above.

Your plant is saved in your browser. On the claude.ai page it's saved to your claude.ai account instead, private to you, so it follows you to other devices.

The page can't ring an alarm or send notifications, so set your phone's alarm too.
